Written Reflection

Why we chose this literary work

We chose The Hour of the Star because Macabea is a character who is almost invisible to the world, but her life still contains dreams, questions, and small moments of happiness. Connecting her story to Brazil and football helps us show that not every star is famous: some stars are hidden, silent, and ignored. This project interprets Macabea as an "invisible player" whose locker room reveals her work, curiosity, identity, small joys, and hope.

Country Connection

Brazil and Clarice Lispector

The novel takes place in Brazil and reflects social inequality, migration, poverty, and urban life. Clarice Lispector uses Macabea to make readers notice a person who would usually be ignored. Our football theme connects with Brazil's global image, but it also asks a deeper question: who gets to be seen as a star?

Responsible Use of AI

AI Was Used

Yes, AI was used in this project. It helped us organize ideas, improve the English wording, generate the first HTML, CSS, and JavaScript structure, and polish the visual presentation. The team selected the literary interpretation, reviewed the content, connected each symbol to Macabea, and is responsible for the final explanation.

Final Message

Not every star is seen at first

Macabea's story reminds us that being quiet does not mean being empty. She may be treated as invisible, but this project gives her a locker, a number, and a place on the field.
